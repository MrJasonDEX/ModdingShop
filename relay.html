BEFORE YOU START

Get the basics ready.

Have these ready before changing your network settings. The exact relay program and game versions you need depend on the method you're following.

Windows PC

Your PC must be connected to the same local network as your router. Use a wired connection if possible.

Console and game

Have Black Ops 2 ready and check that the relay method supports your platform and game version.

Router access

You'll need access to your router settings if the relay method requires port forwarding.

FOLLOW IN ORDER

Your relay setup.

Work through each stage. Router menus differ by manufacturer, so the wording on your screen might not match exactly.

01

Find your PC's local IP address

On your PC, open Command Prompt and run this command. Find the active network adapter, its IPv4 address and the default gateway.

ipconfig
02

Open your router settings

Enter your default gateway address in your browser and sign in to your router. Find Port Forwarding, Virtual Server or a similarly named option.

03

Add the required UDP ports

For the relay method covered by this guide, the port details provided are below. Use your PC's local IPv4 as the destination and confirm the specific relay tool requires these ports.

UDP 3060–3080
UDP 17376

If your router doesn't accept a range, create individual rules only if required by your setup. Don't add unrelated ports.

04

Check Windows Firewall

Make sure Windows hasn't blocked the relay program. If necessary, allow the specific app or create a narrowly scoped rule for the required ports. Keep your firewall enabled.

05

Launch and test the relay

Follow the instructions supplied with your relay tool, check that your game and tool versions are compatible, then test the connection. A port-forwarding rule alone does not guarantee the relay will work.

Important: If your router's WAN address is behind CGNAT or another router, forwarding rules may not be enough. Contact your ISP or check your network setup if you still can't connect.
